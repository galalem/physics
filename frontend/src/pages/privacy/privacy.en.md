# Privacy Policy

_Effective: {effective_date}_

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use Φysics, why we collect it, how we protect it, and the rights you have over it.

## Who we are

Φysics ({domain}) is an educational platform operated by {legal_entity}. In this policy, "we" means {legal_entity} and "you" means the person using the service.

You can reach our team about anything in this policy at [{contact_email}](mailto:{contact_email}).

## What we collect

To create and run your account, we collect the minimum needed to make Φysics work for you:

- Your first name, last name, and email address — you enter these at sign-up.
- A cryptographic hash of your password. We never see or store your password in plain text.
- A session token, stored in a cookie, so you stay logged in between visits.
- Your language preference, stored in your browser.
- Records of the exercises you attempt — which exercise, when, and the outcome.
- Your IP address, briefly, for rate-limiting and abuse prevention.

We do not collect location data, contacts, browsing history, biometric identifiers, or anything from third-party trackers. We do not run advertising and we do not build behavioural profiles.

## Why we collect it

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we rely on two lawful bases:

- **Performance of a contract.** We need your account details to give you access to the service you signed up for.
- **Legitimate interest.** We use IP addresses, session data, and attempt records to keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and improve the exercises over time. We weigh this interest against your rights and use the minimum data needed.

## Cookies

We use one cookie: a session cookie that keeps you logged in. It is `HttpOnly`, `Secure`, and `SameSite=Lax`, and is not shared with any third party.

That is it. No analytics cookies. No advertising cookies. No third-party trackers. Your language preference is kept in your browser's local storage, not in a cookie.

## Who we share it with

No one — for marketing or profiling purposes. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

We do use a mail provider to deliver account emails (verification, password reset). If we ever add another subprocessor, we will list it here before it starts handling your data.

## How long we keep it

As long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is removed within 30 days.

We may keep aggregated, anonymised attempt statistics for research and to improve the exercises. This data cannot be linked back to you.

## Your rights

Under GDPR and equivalent laws in your country, you have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
- Delete your account and your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
- Export your personal data in a portable, machine-readable format.
- Restrict or object to certain processing.
- Lodge a complaint with your national data-protection authority. In the EU, that is the supervisory authority in the country where you live.

You can exercise most of these rights directly from your account settings. For anything else, write to [{contact_email}](mailto:{contact_email}). We respond within 30 days.

## Children and young learners

Φysics is designed for high-school students. Under GDPR, the age at which a young person can consent to online services on their own is 16 by default, but some EU countries set it lower (as low as 13). Outside the EU, other thresholds apply.

If you are below the age of consent where you live, please have a parent or legal guardian read this policy with you and consent on your behalf before creating an account. We do not knowingly process the personal data of children below the age of consent without a parent's involvement.

## Security

Passwords are hashed with argon2id, the algorithm currently recommended by OWASP.

Session cookies are `HttpOnly`, `Secure`, and rotated. All traffic is encrypted in transit with HTTPS. Access to production data is limited to what is strictly needed to operate the service.

If we ever discover a personal-data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify our supervisory authority within 72 hours and inform affected users without undue delay, as required by GDPR.
